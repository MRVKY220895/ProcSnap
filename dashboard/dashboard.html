Export a formatted Word document with custom step headers, descriptions, callout note boxes, expected results, and embedded high-res screenshots.

📊

PowerPoint Presentation (.pptx)

Export a widescreen 16:9 slide deck with custom cover slide, individual step slides, and full-resolution embedded screenshots.
